# Softwares 

When researching softwares utilized in film technologies I found that the website named [Five Day The School ](https://fivedayfilm.com/top-apps-for-filmmakers/) was highly useful since it immediately presented me with various softwares without over explanations of how they work. Softwares that I specifically focused on were Meteo Earth, Shot designer, and Sunseeker. Firstly, Meteo turns mass amounts of complex data into a simple 3d experience that people can utilize without any special training or experience. In shot designer you are able to quickly drag and drop actors, cameras, lights, props,etc onto a 2D canvas instead of wasting large amounts of time through drawing diagrams by hand. Finally, the final software that stood out was the Sunseeker app because it tracks where the sun is at different time periods of the day with 3d augmented tracking for the price of only $12. Although these softwares had there downsides such as certain difficult use, price, glitches, and various other things, they still for the most part highly efficiently which significantly boosts the filming process. Through researching these film production softwares, I learned that an app doesn't have to be the most popular or most excpesnsive for it to be the best, there are various other smaller softwares that work almost exactly the same but for lower prices and even for free.
